# Data licences and attribution

AirBrief **code** is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). Apart from the public-domain World Magnetic Model
coefficients (see "Data shipped with the code" below), AirBrief does **not** ship third-party data
in the repository. Real data packs are downloaded on your own machine by
`python -m seed.datapack --region bengaluru`, and each source keeps its own licence, listed below.
When you publish maps, screenshots or exports that contain this data, include the attribution
lines shown here.

The **synthetic demo pack** (`python -m seed.datapack --synthetic`) and every file produced by
`python -m seed.samples` are generated by AirBrief code. They contain no third-party data and are
covered by the MIT licence. The four aerodrome positions (and VOBL's IATA code) in the synthetic
pack are approximate public facts, not a database extract; its power lines, substation and
assembly venues are invented.

### Mirrors

- **Overpass:** when the primary instance fails or rate-limits, the seed script falls back to the
  mirror above automatically. Re-running the command is safe; downloads resume.
- **Copernicus DEM:** tiles are public COGs on AWS Open Data; no account or key is needed.
- **WorldPop:** if the 1 km India file is unavailable, a GHSL population GeoTIFF (same licence,
  EPSG:4326, people per pixel) can replace `data/datapacks/<name>/population.tif` by hand.

## What the licences mean for you

- **ODbL (OpenStreetMap):** you may use, share and adapt the data, but you must attribute it and,
  if you publicly distribute an *adapted database*, share it under ODbL. Maps and images produced
  from it ("Produced Works") need only the attribution.
- **CC BY 4.0 (WorldPop, GHSL):** free use with attribution; indicate changes (AirBrief clips and
  resamples the grid).
- **Copernicus DEM:** free use with the attribution above; do not imply endorsement.
- **Public domain (OurAirports):** no conditions; attribution is a courtesy.
- **Public domain (WMM2025, NOAA NCEI / BGS):** no conditions; attribution is a courtesy. Replace the
  coefficients only with a later official release (the file is validated against NOAA's test values).

## Disclaimers

- Airspace zones produced by AirBrief are **illustrative** rules applied to aerodrome reference
  points. They are not an official airspace map and are not a substitute for DGCA Digital Sky or any
  other authority's authorisation.
- Zone files you import (ED-269 JSON, ED-318 GeoJSON, your own layers) keep their own licences:
  AirBrief bundles none. National files are published under various terms (Luxembourg's under
  CC0, others under national open-data licences); check them before sharing exports that contain
  their zones.
- Population grids are modelled estimates and may be outdated, and they do not include event
  crowds. Ground-risk outputs are SORA 2.5 Step 2 / Step 8 operational-volume *evidence*, not a
  GRC, ARC or SAIL and not the assessment itself.
- The intrinsic ground-risk table reproduces JARUS SORA 2.5 Main Body, Table 2 (columns up to
  1 m/25 m/s, 3 m/35 m/s, 8 m/75 m/s, 20 m/120 m/s, 40 m/200 m/s), and the density grid follows
  Table 4. Verify values against the official JARUS publication before use.
- OpenStreetMap assembly venues often lack a `capacity` tag ("capacity unknown"); scheduled events
  must be entered by the user.
- Environment evidence: WMM2025 is a main-field model (it leaves out local anomalies, magnetic storms
  and nearby steel); OpenStreetMap power lines are often incomplete and their voltage tags are often
  missing, so the power-line list is proximity evidence, not an interference prediction.
